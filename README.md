# DataCapstone

Capstone Project Proposal
F1 Safety Car Impact Analysis
1. Abstract
I want to study how much Safety Cars actually change the result of a Formula 1 race. When a
Safety Car comes out, all the drivers get bunched together and there are new opportunities to
gain positions. But does it really help some teams more than others? My project will look at all
the race data from 2018 until now and measure which drivers and teams get the biggest
advantage from Safety Car moments. I will make an interactive dashboard so people can explore
the data themselves and see which races were actually decided by Safety Car luck versus pure
driver skill.
2. What Are the Goals?
My main research question is: How much does a Safety Car change the result of an F1 race?
Which teams and drivers benefit the most?
What I want to find:
• How many positions do drivers gain or lose on average when a Safety Car happens
• Which racing tracks are affected more by Safety Cars (for example: Monaco versus
Monza)
• Whether the timing of your pit stop during a Safety Car makes a big difference to your
final result
• Specific races where the Safety Car directly decided who won
• Create clear charts and graphs that tell this story
What I will deliver:
1. 2. 3. 4. An interactive website where people can explore and filter the data themselves
All my code on GitHub so people can see exactly how I did it
A presentation slide deck with my main findings
A complete explanation of my process and methodology
