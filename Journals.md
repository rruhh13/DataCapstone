## Week 1

For the first week, I was just exploring different ideas for my project. Because most of my previous experience has been with supply chain and some economics data, I wanted to work with something completely different.

I have always been into cars, and I enjoy watching Formula 1 races, so I started looking at F1 data projects. The Safety Car topic caught my attention because a race can change a lot after just one Safety Car. I thought it would be cool to find out if the data actually shows which drivers and teams benefit from these situations.

So for now, I decided to go with the F1 Safety Car Impact Analysis project and see where I can take it.
